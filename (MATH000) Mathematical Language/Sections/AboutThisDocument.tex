\sectionmark{About This Document}

Mathematical Language records the notation and writing conventions adopted here. It is guided by deliberate personal preferences together with established mathematical practice; when no personal convention has been fixed, standard mathematical usage is the default.

Part I establishes general notation and writing conventions; Part II records subject-specific notation and conventions.

This document is non-exhaustive. Alternative notations are listed in preferred order: the first is used by default, while later forms remain acceptable when context or established usage favors them.
