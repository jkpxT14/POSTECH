\sectionmark{About This Document}

Mathematical Language records the notation and writing conventions adopted here. It is guided by deliberate personal preferences together with established mathematical practice; when no personal convention has been fixed, standard mathematical usage is the default.

This document is intentionally non-exhaustive and may be extended as new subjects and notation are encountered. A displayed example or notation-table entry gives a standard or preferred form, not an exhaustive grammar for every related mathematical expression, unless explicitly stated otherwise. When multiple alternative notations for the same object or purpose are listed, they are given in preferred order unless stated otherwise: the primary notation is used by default, while listed secondary notation remains acceptable when the surrounding context, an adopted textbook or lecture note, an established field convention, or the notation of a given problem makes it preferable. The absence of a notation from this document does not by itself make that notation invalid.

When notation not covered here is needed, an established standard should be used when suitable; otherwise, a clear, mathematically coherent, and unambiguous notation appropriate to the context may be introduced. Useful choices may later be incorporated into this document. These conventions should be applied with mathematical judgment rather than mechanically: explicitly stated conventions should be respected, while established field-specific usage, semantic distinctions, and context take precedence over superficial uniformity.
