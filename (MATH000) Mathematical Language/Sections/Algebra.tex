\section{Algebra}

$G,H$ usually denote groups, $R,S$ rings, $I,J$ ideals, $M,N$ modules, and $F,K,L$ fields. Modules are left modules unless otherwise stated.

\begin{notationtable}
$G$ & Group. \\
$e,\ 1_G$ & Identity element of a group. \\
$g^{-1}$ & Inverse of $g\in G$. \\
$|G|$ & Order (cardinality) of $G$. \\
$\ord(g),\ |g|$ & Order of the element $g$. \\
$\langle S\rangle$ & Subgroup generated by $S\subseteq G$. \\
$\langle g\rangle$ & Cyclic subgroup generated by $g$. \\
$H\le G$ & $H$ is a subgroup of $G$. \\
$H\trianglelefteq G$ & $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. \\
$gH,\ Hg$ & Left and right cosets of $H$ in $G$. \\
$[G:H]$ & Index of $H$ in $G$. \\
$G/H$ & Quotient group, for $H\trianglelefteq G$. \\
$\ker\varphi$ & Kernel of the homomorphism $\varphi$. \\
$\im\varphi$ & Image of the homomorphism $\varphi$. \\
$\Aut(G)$ & Automorphism group of $G$. \\
$\Inn(G)$ & Inner automorphism group of $G$. \\
$Z(G)$ & Center of $G$. \\
$C_G(A),\ C_G(x)$ & Centralizer in $G$ of $A\subseteq G$ or $x\in G$. \\
$N_G(A),\ N_G(H)$ & Normalizer in $G$ of $A\subseteq G$ or $H\le G$. \\
$[x,y]\coloneqq x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$ & Commutator of $x$ and $y$. \\
$[G,G],\ G'$ & Commutator (derived) subgroup of $G$. \\
$\Syl_p(G)$ & Set of Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$. \\
$n_p$ & Number of Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$. \\
$G\curvearrowright X$ & Action of $G$ on $X$. \\
$g\cdot x$ & Action of $g\in G$ on $x\in X$. \\
$G\cdot x$ & Orbit of $x$ under the action of $G$. \\
$\Stab_G(x),\ G_x$ & Stabilizer of $x$ in $G$. \\
$G\times H$ & Direct product of groups. \\
$N\rtimes_{\varphi}H$ & Semidirect product determined by $\varphi\colon H\to\Aut(N)$, with $N$ the normal factor. \\
$\langle S\mid R\rangle$ & Group presentation with generators $S$ and relations $R$. \\
$C_n$ & Cyclic group of order $n$. \\
$D_n$ & Dihedral group of symmetries of a regular $n$-gon; $|D_n|=2n$. \\
$S_n,\ \mathfrak S_n$ & Symmetric group on $n$ symbols. \\
$A_n,\ \mathfrak A_n$ & Alternating group on $n$ symbols. \\
$V_4$ & Klein four-group. \\
$Q_8$ & Quaternion group of order $8$. \\
$\GL_n(F)$ & General linear group over $F$. \\
$\SL_n(F)$ & Special linear group over $F$. \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
$R$ & Ring. \\
$0_R,\ 1_R$ & Additive and multiplicative identities of $R$, when disambiguation is useful. \\
$R^\times$ & Group of units of $R$. \\
$I,\ J$ & Ideals of $R$, when the ambient ring is clear. \\
$\mathfrak p,\ \mathfrak q$ & Prime ideals. \\
$\mathfrak m,\ \mathfrak n$ & Maximal ideals. \\
$(a)$ & Principal ideal generated by $a$. \\
$(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ & Ideal generated by $a_1,\dots,a_n$. \\
$I+J$ & Sum of the ideals $I$ and $J$. \\
$IJ$ & Product of the ideals $I$ and $J$. \\
$R/I$ & Quotient ring. \\
$R[x],\ R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ & Polynomial rings over $R$ in one or several variables. \\
$\deg f$ & Degree of the polynomial $f$. \\
$M_n(R)$ & Ring of $n\times n$ matrices over $R$. \\
$R[G]$ & Group ring of $G$ over $R$. \\
$\Frac(R)$ & Fraction field of the integral domain $R$. \\
$\charac R$ & Characteristic of $R$. \\
$\Hquat$ & Quaternion division algebra over $\R$. \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
$M,\ N$ & Left $R$-modules, unless otherwise stated. \\
${}_RM,\ M_R$ & Left and right $R$-modules, when the side is emphasized. \\
$M/N$ & Quotient module, for a submodule $N$ of $M$. \\
$M\oplus N$ & Direct sum of modules. \\
$\displaystyle\bigoplus_{i\in I} M_i$ & Direct sum of an indexed family of modules. \\
$R^n$ & Free $R$-module of rank $n$. \\
$\Hom_R(M,N)$ & Set of $R$-module homomorphisms from $M$ to $N$. \\
$\End_R(M)$ & Endomorphism ring of the $R$-module $M$. \\
$\Aut_R(M)$ & Automorphism group of the $R$-module $M$. \\
$\Ann_R(M)$ & Annihilator of the $R$-module $M$. \\
$M\otimes_R N$ & Tensor product over $R$, with the required right and left module structures understood. \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
$K/F$ & Field extension of $F$ by $K$. \\
$[K:F]$ & Degree of the field extension $K/F$. \\
$F(\alpha),\ F(\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n)$ & Fields generated over $F$ by the indicated elements. \\
$F[\alpha]$ & Subring (equivalently, $F$-subalgebra) generated by $F$ and $\alpha$. \\
$m_{\alpha,F}(x)$ & Minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $F$. \\
$\overline F$ & Algebraic closure of $F$. \\
$\Gal(L/K)$ & Galois group of $L/K$. \\
$L^H$ & Fixed field of $H\le\Gal(L/K)$. \\
$\Tr_{L/K}(\alpha)$ & Field trace of $\alpha\in L$ from $L$ to $K$. \\
$\FieldNorm_{L/K}(\alpha)$ & Field norm of $\alpha\in L$ from $L$ to $K$. \\
$\trdeg_K L$ & Transcendence degree of $L$ over $K$. \\
\end{notationtable}
