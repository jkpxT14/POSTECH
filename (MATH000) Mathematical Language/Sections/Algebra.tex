\section{Algebra}

Capital letters such as $G,H$ denote groups, $R,S$ rings, and $F,K,L$ fields when no conflicting local notation is established.

\begin{notationtable}
$G$ & Group. \\
$e,\ 1_G$ & Identity element of a group. \\
$|G|$ & Order of a finite group. \\
$\langle S\rangle$ & Subgroup generated by $S$. \\
$H\le G$ & $H$ is a subgroup of $G$. \\
$H\trianglelefteq G$ & $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. \\
$G/H$ & Quotient group. \\
$\ker\varphi$ & Kernel of $\varphi$. \\
$\im\varphi$ & Image of $\varphi$. \\
$\Aut(G)$ & Automorphism group of $G$. \\
$Z(G)$ & Center of $G$. \\
$C_G(x)$ & Centralizer of $x$ in $G$. \\
$\GL_n(F)$ & General linear group over $F$. \\
$\SL_n(F)$ & Special linear group over $F$. \\
$S_n,\ \mathfrak S_n$ & Symmetric group on $n$ symbols. \\
$A_n,\ \mathfrak A_n$ & Alternating group on $n$ symbols. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$R$ & Ring. \\
$I\trianglelefteq R$ & $I$ is an ideal of $R$. \\
$R/I$ & Quotient ring. \\
$F$ & Base field in an algebraic context. \\
$K/F$ & Field extension of $F$ by $K$. \\
$F[x]$ & Polynomial ring in $x$ over $F$. \\
$\deg f$ & Degree of a polynomial $f$. \\
$\charac F$ & Characteristic of $F$. \\
$\Gal(L/K)$ & Galois group of $L/K$. \\
$\trdeg_K L$ & Transcendence degree of $L$ over $K$. \\
\end{notationtable}
