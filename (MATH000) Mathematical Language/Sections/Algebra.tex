\section{Algebra}

$G,H$ usually denote groups, $R,S$ rings, $I,J$ ideals, $M,N$ modules, and $F,K,L$ fields. Rings are associative and have an identity unless otherwise stated; commutativity is not assumed. Ring homomorphisms preserve multiplicative identities. Unless otherwise stated, ideals are two-sided. The zero ring is allowed, while fields and division rings are nonzero. Modules are left modules unless otherwise stated.

\begin{notationtable}
$G$ & Group \\
$e,\ 1_G$ & Identity element of a group \\
$g^{-1}$ & Inverse of $g\in G$ \\
$|G|$ & Order (cardinality) of $G$ \\
$\ord(g),\ |g|$ & Order of the element $g$ \\
$\langle S\rangle$ & Subgroup generated by $S\subseteq G$ \\
$\langle g\rangle$ & Cyclic subgroup generated by $g$ \\
$H\le G$ & $H$ is a subgroup of $G$. \\
$H\trianglelefteq G$ & $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. \\
$gH,\ Hg$ & Left and right cosets of $H$ in $G$ \\
$[G:H]$ & Index of $H$ in $G$ \\
$G/H$ & Set of left cosets of $H\le G$ in $G$; a quotient group when $H\trianglelefteq G$ \\
$\ker\varphi$ & Kernel of the homomorphism $\varphi$ \\
$\im\varphi$ & Image of the homomorphism $\varphi$ \\
$\Hom(G,H)$ & Set of group homomorphisms from $G$ to $H$ \\
$\Aut(G)$ & Automorphism group of $G$ \\
$\Inn(G)$ & Inner automorphism group of $G$ \\
$\Out(G)$ & Outer automorphism group $\Aut(G)/\Inn(G)$ \\
$Z(G)$ & Center of $G$ \\
$C_G(A),\ C_G(x)$ & Centralizer in $G$ of $A\subseteq G$ or $x\in G$ \\
$N_G(A),\ N_G(H)$ & Normalizer in $G$ of $A\subseteq G$ or $H\le G$ \\
$g^G=\{hgh^{-1}\mid h\in G\}$ & Conjugacy class of $g\in G$ \\
$[x,y]\coloneqq x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$ & Commutator of $x$ and $y$ \\
$[G,G],\ G'$ & Commutator (derived) subgroup of $G$ \\
$G^{\mathrm{ab}}=G/[G,G]$ & Abelianization of $G$ \\
$\Syl_p(G)$ & Set of Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$ \\
$n_p$ & Number of Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$ \\
$G\curvearrowright X$ & Action of $G$ on $X$ \\
$g\cdot x$ & Action of $g\in G$ on $x\in X$ \\
$G\cdot x,\ \Orb_G(x),\ \Orb(x)$ & Orbit of $x$ under the action of $G$ \\
$G\backslash X,\ X/G$ & Set of orbits of a left $G$-action on $X$; $X/G$ is also used when the action convention is clear \\
$\Stab_G(x),\ G_x$ & Stabilizer of $x$ in $G$ \\
$\Fix(g),\ X^g$ & Fixed-point set $\{x\in X\mid g\cdot x=x\}$ of $g\in G$ acting on $X$ \\
$X^H$ & Common fixed-point set $\{x\in X\mid h\cdot x=x\text{ for all }h\in H\}$ of $H\le G$ \\
$G\times H$ & Direct product of groups \\
$N\rtimes_{\varphi}H$ & Semidirect product determined by $\varphi\colon H\to\Aut(N)$, with $N$ the normal factor \\
$\langle S\mid R\rangle$ & Group presentation with generators $S$ and relations $R$ \\
$C_n$ & Cyclic group of order $n$ \\
$D_n$ & Dihedral group of symmetries of a regular $n$-gon; $|D_n|=2n$ (alternative convention: $D_{2n}$) \\
$\Sym(X)$ & Symmetric group of all permutations of the set $X$ \\
$S_n,\ \mathfrak S_n$ & Symmetric group on $n$ symbols \\
$(a_1\,a_2\,\dots\,a_k)$ & Cycle notation for a permutation sending $a_i$ to $a_{i+1}$ for $i<k$ and $a_k$ to $a_1$ \\
$A_n,\ \mathfrak A_n$ & Alternating group on $n$ symbols \\
$V_4$ & Klein four-group \\
$Q_8$ & Quaternion group of order $8$ \\
$\GL_n(F)$ & General linear group over $F$ \\
$\SL_n(F)$ & Special linear group over $F$ \\
$\mathrm O(n)$ & Orthogonal group of $n\times n$ real matrices \\
$\mathrm{SO}(n)$ & Special orthogonal group; matrices in $\mathrm O(n)$ with determinant $1$ \\
$\mathrm U(n)$ & Unitary group of $n\times n$ complex matrices \\
$\mathrm{SU}(n)$ & Special unitary group; matrices in $\mathrm U(n)$ with determinant $1$ \\
$R$ & Ring \\
$0_R,\ 1_R$ & Additive and multiplicative identities of $R$ \\
$R^\times$ & Group of units of $R$ \\
$I,\ J,\ \mathfrak a,\ \mathfrak b$ & Ideals of $R$; unless otherwise stated, ideals are two-sided \\
$\mathfrak p,\ \mathfrak q$ & Prime ideals \\
$\Spec(R)$ & Prime spectrum of a commutative ring $R$: the set of its prime ideals \\
$\mathfrak m,\ \mathfrak n$ & Maximal ideals \\
$\sqrt I$ & Radical of the ideal $I$ in a commutative ring $R$: $\{r\in R\mid r^n\in I\text{ for some }n\ge1\}$ \\
$(a)$ & Two-sided ideal generated by $a$; in a commutative ring, the usual principal ideal \\
$(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ & Two-sided ideal generated by $a_1,\dots,a_n$ \\
$I+J$ & Sum of the ideals $I$ and $J$ \\
$IJ$ & Product of the ideals $I$ and $J$ \\
$R/I$ & Quotient ring \\
$R[x],\ R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ & Polynomial rings over $R$ in commuting central indeterminates \\
$R[\alpha]$ & Subring generated by $R$ and $\alpha$ in a commutative $R$-algebra \\
$\deg f$ & Degree of the polynomial $f$ \\
$\disc(f)$ & Discriminant of the polynomial $f$ \\
$M_n(R)$ & Ring of $n\times n$ matrices over $R$ \\
$R[G]$ & Group ring of $G$ over $R$ \\
$\Frac(R)$ & Fraction field of the integral domain $R$ \\
$\charac R$ & Characteristic of $R$ \\
$\Hquat$ & Quaternion division algebra over $\R$ \\
$M,\ N$ & Left $R$-modules, unless otherwise stated \\
${}_RM,\ M_R$ & Left and right $R$-modules, when the side is emphasized \\
$M/N$ & Quotient module, for a submodule $N$ of $M$ \\
$M\oplus N$ & Direct sum of modules \\
$\displaystyle\bigoplus_{i\in\Lambda} M_i$ & Direct sum of an indexed family of modules, with index set $\Lambda$ \\
$R^n$ & Free $R$-module of rank $n$ \\
$\Hom_R(M,N)$ & Set of $R$-module homomorphisms from $M$ to $N$ \\
$\End_R(M)$ & Endomorphism ring of the $R$-module $M$ \\
$\Aut_R(M)$ & Automorphism group of the $R$-module $M$ \\
$\Ann_R(M)$ & Annihilator of the $R$-module $M$ \\
$M_R\otimes_R {}_RN,\ M\otimes_R N$ & Tensor product over $R$; the first notation emphasizes that $M$ is a right $R$-module and $N$ a left $R$-module. The side distinction is normally suppressed when $R$ is commutative or the required module structures are otherwise clear \\
$K/F$ & Field extension of $F$ by $K$ \\
$[K:F]$ & Degree of the field extension $K/F$ \\
$F(\alpha),\ F(\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n)$ & Fields generated over $F$ by the indicated elements \\
$F[\alpha]$ & Subring (equivalently, $F$-subalgebra) generated by $F$ and $\alpha$ \\
$m_{\alpha,F}(x)$ & Minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $F$ \\
$\overline F$ & Algebraic closure of $F$ \\
$\Gal(L/K)$ & Galois group of $L/K$ \\
$L^H$ & Fixed field of $H\le\Gal(L/K)$ \\
$\Tr_{L/K}(\alpha)$ & Field trace of $\alpha\in L$ from $L$ to $K$ \\
$\FieldNorm_{L/K}(\alpha)$ & Field norm of $\alpha\in L$ from $L$ to $K$ \\
$\trdeg_K L$ & Transcendence degree of $L$ over $K$ \\
\end{notationtable}
