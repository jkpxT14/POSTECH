\section{Calculus and Analysis}

The differential $\dd$ is upright; $\varepsilon$ is the default small positive quantity in analysis.

\begin{notationtable}
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a} f(x)$ & Limit as $x$ approaches $a$. \\
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a^-} f(x)$ & Left-hand limit as $x$ approaches $a$. \\*
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a^+} f(x)$ & Right-hand limit as $x$ approaches $a$. \\
$\min A$ & Minimum of $A$. \\*
$\max A$ & Maximum of $A$. \\
$\inf A$ & Infimum of $A$. \\*
$\sup A$ & Supremum of $A$. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$f'(a)$ & First derivative of $f$ at $a$. \\
$f^{(n)}(a)$ & $n$th derivative of $f$ at $a$. \\
$\displaystyle\frac{\dd y}{\dd x}$ & Derivative of $y$ with respect to $x$. \\
$\displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i},\ \partial_i f$ & Partial derivative with respect to $x_i$. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,\dd x$ & Definite integral over $[a,b]$. \\
$\displaystyle\iint_D f\,\dd A$ & Double integral over $D$. \\
$\displaystyle\iiint_\Omega f\,\dd V$ & Triple integral over $\Omega$. \\
$\displaystyle\oint_C f(z)\,\dd z$ & Contour integral over a closed curve $C$. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_k$ & Finite sum. \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k$ & Infinite series. \\
$\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{n} a_k$ & Finite product. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$e^x,\ \exp x$ & Exponential function. \\
$\ln x,\ \log_e x$ & Logarithm to base $e$. \\
$\lg x,\ \log_{10} x$ & Logarithm to base $10$. \\
$\lb x,\ \log_2 x$ & Logarithm to base $2$. \\
$\log_a x$ & Logarithm to base $a$. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\sin x$ & Sine. \\*
$\cos x$ & Cosine. \\*
$\tan x$ & Tangent. \\*
$\csc x$ & Cosecant. \\*
$\sec x$ & Secant. \\*
$\cot x$ & Cotangent. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\arcsin x,\ \sin^{-1}x$ & Principal inverse sine. \\*
$\arccos x,\ \cos^{-1}x$ & Principal inverse cosine. \\*
$\arctan x,\ \tan^{-1}x$ & Principal inverse tangent. \\*
$\arccsc x,\ \csc^{-1}x$ & Principal inverse cosecant. \\*
$\arcsec x,\ \sec^{-1}x$ & Principal inverse secant. \\*
$\arccot x,\ \cot^{-1}x$ & Principal inverse cotangent. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\sinh x$ & Hyperbolic sine. \\*
$\cosh x$ & Hyperbolic cosine. \\*
$\tanh x$ & Hyperbolic tangent. \\*
$\csch x$ & Hyperbolic cosecant. \\*
$\sech x$ & Hyperbolic secant. \\*
$\coth x$ & Hyperbolic cotangent. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\begin{aligned} &\arcsinh x,\ \arsinh x,\\ &\sinh^{-1}x\end{aligned}$ & Principal inverse hyperbolic sine. \\*
$\begin{aligned} &\arccosh x,\ \arcosh x,\\ &\cosh^{-1}x\end{aligned}$ & Principal inverse hyperbolic cosine. \\*
$\begin{aligned} &\arctanh x,\ \artanh x,\\ &\tanh^{-1}x\end{aligned}$ & Principal inverse hyperbolic tangent. \\*
$\begin{aligned} &\arccsch x,\ \arcsch x,\\ &\csch^{-1}x\end{aligned}$ & Principal inverse hyperbolic cosecant. \\*
$\begin{aligned} &\arcsech x,\ \arsech x,\\ &\sech^{-1}x\end{aligned}$ & Principal inverse hyperbolic secant. \\*
$\begin{aligned} &\arccoth x,\ \arcoth x,\\ &\coth^{-1}x\end{aligned}$ & Principal inverse hyperbolic cotangent. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$\Gamma(z)$ & Gamma function. \\
$B(x,y)$ & Beta function. \\
$\zeta(s)$ & Riemann zeta function. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$D\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{a})$ & Total derivative; Jacobian matrix in coordinates. \\
$\Jac(\mathbf{F})$ & Jacobian determinant. \\
$\Hess f$ & Hessian matrix of $f$. \\
$\nabla f$ & Gradient of $f$. \\
$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}$ & Divergence of $\mathbf{F}$. \\
$\nabla\times\mathbf{F}$ & Curl of $\mathbf{F}$. \\
$\Delta f$ & Laplacian of $f$. \\
$\mathbf{r}(t)$ & Parametrized curve. \\
$\dd s$ & Element of arc length. \\
$\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf{F}\cdot\dd\mathbf{r}$ & Line integral along $C$. \\
$\displaystyle\iint_S\mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{n}\,\dd S$ & Flux integral through $S$. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$x_n\to x$ & Convergence of a sequence. \\
$a_n\nearrow L$ & Monotone increasing convergence to $L$. \\*
$a_n\searrow L$ & Monotone decreasing convergence to $L$. \\
$\limsup a_n$ & Limit superior. \\*
$\liminf a_n$ & Limit inferior. \\
$f_n\to f,\ f_n\ptwlabelto f$ & Pointwise convergence. \\*
$f_n\rightrightarrows f,\ f_n\uniflabelto f$ & Uniform convergence. \\
$f_n\aeto f$ & Convergence almost everywhere. \\
$C^k(I)$ & $k$ times continuously differentiable functions on $I$. \\*
$C^\infty(I)$ & Smooth functions on $I$. \\
$\|f\|_\infty$ & Supremum norm. \\
$B_r(x)$ & Open ball of radius $r$ centered at $x$. \\*
$\overline{B}_r(x)$ & Closed ball of radius $r$ centered at $x$. \\
\addlinespace[0.26em]
$z=x+iy$ & Complex number with real part $x$ and imaginary part $y$. \\
$\RePart z$ & Real part of $z$. \\
$\ImPart z$ & Imaginary part of $z$; distinct from lowercase $\im$ for image. \\
$\overline{z}$ & Complex conjugate. \\
$|z|$ & Complex modulus. \\
$\arg z$ & Argument of $z$. \\
$\Res(f;a),\ \operatorname{Res}_{z=a}f(z)$ & Residue of $f$ at $a$. \\
\end{notationtable}
