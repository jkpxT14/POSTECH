\section{Calculus and Analysis}

The differential $\dd$ is upright; $\varepsilon$ is the default small positive quantity in analysis.

\begin{notationtable}
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a}f(x), \ f(a^{\pm})$ & Two-sided limit at $a$; $f(a^{\pm})$ is used only for a finite limit \\
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x), \ f(a^{-})$ & Left-hand limit at $a$ \\*
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x), \ f(a^{+})$ & Right-hand limit at $a$ \\
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x), \ f(\infty)$ & Limit as $x\to\infty$; $f(\infty)$ is used only for a finite limit \\
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-\infty}f(x), \ f(-\infty)$ & Limit as $x\to-\infty$; $f(-\infty)$ is used only for a finite limit \\
$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=\pm\infty$ & Infinite limit at $a$ \\
$\min A$ & Minimum of $A$ \\*
$\max A$ & Maximum of $A$ \\
$\inf A$ & Infimum of $A$ \\*
$\sup A$ & Supremum of $A$ \\
$\displaystyle\argmin_{x\in A}f(x)$ & Set of minimizers of $f$ over $A$ \\*
$\displaystyle\argmax_{x\in A}f(x)$ & Set of maximizers of $f$ over $A$ \\
$\overline{\R}=\R\cup\{-\infty, +\infty\}$ & Extended real line \\
$\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n=x, \ x_n\to x$ & Convergence of a sequence \\
$(x_{n_k})$ & Subsequence of the sequence $(x_n)$ \\
$a_n\nearrow L$ & Monotone increasing convergence to $L$ \\*
$a_n\searrow L$ & Monotone decreasing convergence to $L$ \\
$\displaystyle\limsup_{n\to\infty}a_n$ & Limit superior of the sequence $(a_n)$ \\*
$\displaystyle\liminf_{n\to\infty}a_n$ & Limit inferior of the sequence $(a_n)$ \\
$f(x)\sim g(x)$ & Asymptotic equivalence: $f(x)/g(x)\to1$ in the stated limiting regime \\
$f_n\ptwto f, \ f_n\ptwlabelto f$ & Pointwise convergence of $f_n$ to $f$ \\*
$f_n\unifto f, \ f_n\uniflabelto f$ & Uniform convergence \\
$f_n\aeto f$ & Convergence almost everywhere \\
$f_n\Lpto{p}f$ & Convergence in $L^p$ \\
$x_n\rightharpoonup x$ & Weak convergence \\
$x_n^*\overset{*}{\rightharpoonup}x^*$ & Weak-* convergence in a continuous dual space \\
$f'(a)$ & First derivative of $f$ at $a$ \\
$f^{(n)}(a)$ & $n$th derivative of $f$ at $a$ \\
$\displaystyle\frac{\dd y}{\dd x}$ & Derivative of $y$ with respect to $x$ \\[0.50em]
$\displaystyle\frac{\dd^n y}{\dd x^n}$ & $n$th derivative of $y$ with respect to $x$ in Leibniz notation \\[0.50em]
$\displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}, \ \partial_i f, \ f_{x_i}$ & Partial derivative with respect to $x_i$ \\[0.50em]
$\begin{aligned}&\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_j\partial x_i}, \ \partial_j\partial_i f,\\ &\partial_{ji}f, \ f_{x_i x_j}\end{aligned}$ & Mixed partial derivative, first with respect to $x_i$ and then $x_j$ \\[0.14em]
$D_{\mathbf{v}}f(\mathbf{a}), \ \partial_{\mathbf{v}}f(\mathbf{a})$ & Directional derivative of $f$ at $\mathbf{a}$ along $\mathbf{v}$ \\
$\displaystyle\dd f=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}\,\dd x_i$ & Total differential of a scalar function of $n$ variables \\
$\alpha=(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_n)\in\Nzero^n$ & Multi-index \\
$|\alpha|=\alpha_1+\cdots+\alpha_n$ & Order of a multi-index \\
$\alpha!=\alpha_1!\cdots\alpha_n!$ & Factorial of a multi-index \\
$\mathbf{x}^\alpha=x_1^{\alpha_1}\cdots x_n^{\alpha_n}$ & Multi-index monomial for $\mathbf{x}=(x_1, \dots, x_n)$ \\
$D^\alpha f, \ \partial^\alpha f$ & Mixed partial derivative specified by the multi-index $\alpha$ \\
$Df(a)$ & Total (Fr\'echet) derivative of $f$ at $a$, as a linear map \\
$\mathbf{J}_{\mathbf{F}}(\mathbf{a}), \ D\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{a})$ & Jacobian matrix of $\mathbf{F}$ at $\mathbf{a}$; $D\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{a})$ denotes the total derivative, identified here with its matrix in the standard bases \\
$\det(\mathbf{J}_{\mathbf{F}}(\mathbf{a})), \ \det(D\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{a}))$ & Jacobian determinant of $\mathbf{F}$ at $\mathbf{a}$, for a square Jacobian matrix \\
$\Hess f$ & Hessian matrix of $f$ \\
$\nabla f, \ \operatorname{grad}f$ & Gradient of $f$ \\
$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}, \ \operatorname{div}\mathbf{F}$ & Divergence of $\mathbf{F}$ \\
$\nabla\times\mathbf{F}, \ \operatorname{curl}\mathbf{F}$ & Curl of $\mathbf{F}$ \\
$\Delta f=\nabla\cdot\nabla f$ & Euclidean Laplacian of $f$ under the stated sign convention \\
$\displaystyle\int f(x)\,\dd x$ & Indefinite integral of $f$ \\[0.10em]
$\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x)\,\dd x$ & Definite integral over $[a, b]$ \\[0.10em]
$\left.F(x)\right|_a^b, \ [F(x)]_a^b$ & Evaluation at the endpoints: $F(b)-F(a)$ \\[0.10em]
$\displaystyle\int_a^\infty f(x)\,\dd x, \ \int_{-\infty}^b f(x)\,\dd x$ & Improper integrals over unbounded intervals; also $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,\dd x$ \\[0.10em]
$\displaystyle\int_a^b f\,\dd\alpha$ & Riemann--Stieltjes integral of $f$ over $[a, b]$ with respect to $\alpha$ \\
$P=\{x_0, \dots, x_n\}$ & Partition of $[a, b]$, with $a=x_0<\cdots<x_n=b$ \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^n f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$ & Tagged Riemann sum with sample points $\xi_i$ and subinterval widths $\Delta x_i$ \\
$\displaystyle\iint_D f\,\dd A$ & Double integral over $D$ \\[0.60em]
$\displaystyle\iiint_\Omega f\,\dd V$ & Triple integral over $\Omega$ \\[0.60em]
$\mathbf{r}(t)$ & Parametrized curve \\
$\dd s$ & Element of arc length \\
$\displaystyle\int_C f\,\dd s$ & Scalar line integral along $C$ with respect to arc length \\[0.24em]
$\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf{F}\cdot\dd\mathbf{r}$ & Line integral of the vector field $\mathbf{F}$ along $C$ \\[0.24em]
$\displaystyle\iint_S f\,\dd S$ & Scalar surface integral over $S$ \\[0.24em]
$\displaystyle\oiint_S$ & Surface integral over a closed surface $S$ \\[0.24em]
$\displaystyle\iint_S\mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{n}\,\dd S$ & Flux integral through $S$ \\[0.24em]
$\displaystyle\int_C f(z)\,\dd z$ & Complex line integral along $C$ \\[0.60em]
$\displaystyle\oint_C$ & Integral along a closed curve $C$ \\[0.24em]
$\Ind(\gamma, z)$ & Winding number of the closed curve $\gamma$ about $z$ \\
$-C$ & The contour $C$ with the opposite orientation \\
$\gamma^{-1}$ & Reversed parametrized path $\gamma$ \\
$\PV, \ \operatorname{P.V.}$ & Principal value (in particular, Cauchy principal value) \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} a_k$ & Finite sum \\[0.10em]
$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} a_k$ & Infinite series \\[0.10em]
$\displaystyle s_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k$ & Partial sum of a series \\
$\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{n} a_k$ & Finite product \\[0.10em]
$\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{\infty}a_k$ & Infinite product \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_n(x-a)^n$ & Power series centered at $a$ \\[0.10em]
$R$ & Radius of convergence of a power series \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}a_n(z-z_0)^n$ & Laurent series centered at $z_0$ \\[0.10em]
$T_n(x)$ & $n$th Taylor polynomial of $f$ about $a$: $\displaystyle T_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k$ \\*
$R_n(x)=f(x)-T_n(x)$ & Taylor remainder \\*
$\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k$ & Taylor series of $f$ about $a$ \\
$\widehat f(n)$ & $n$th complex Fourier coefficient of a $2\pi$-periodic function: $\displaystyle\widehat f(n)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x)e^{-inx}\,\dd x$ \\*
$\displaystyle f(x)\sim\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\widehat f(n)e^{inx}$ & Complex Fourier series of a $2\pi$-periodic function; $\sim$ denotes Fourier expansion here, not asymptotic equivalence \\
$S_Nf$ & $N$th symmetric Fourier partial sum: $\displaystyle S_Nf(x)=\sum_{n=-N}^{N}\widehat f(n)e^{inx}$ \\
$\sigma_Nf$ & $N$th Fej\`er mean: $\displaystyle\sigma_Nf(x)=\frac{1}{N+1}\sum_{k=0}^{N}S_kf(x)$ \\
$e^x, \ \exp x$ & Exponential function \\
$\ln x, \ \log_e x$ & Natural logarithm of a positive real number \\
$\lg x, \ \log_{10} x$ & Logarithm to base $10$ \\
$\lb x, \ \log_2 x$ & Logarithm to base $2$ \\
$\log_a x$ & Logarithm to base $a$ \\
$W_k(z)$ & $k$th branch of the Lambert $W$-function; $W_0$ is the principal branch \\
$\sin x$ & Sine \\
$\cos x$ & Cosine \\
$\tan x$ & Tangent \\
$\csc x$ & Cosecant \\
$\sec x$ & Secant \\
$\cot x$ & Cotangent \\
$\arcsin x, \ \sin^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse sine, $[-1, 1]\to[-\pi/2, \pi/2]$ \\
$\arccos x, \ \cos^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse cosine, $[-1, 1]\to[0, \pi]$ \\
$\arctan x, \ \tan^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse tangent, $\R\to(-\pi/2, \pi/2)$ \\
$\arccsc x, \ \csc^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse cosecant, with domain $\R\setminus(-1, 1)$ and range $[-\pi/2, 0)\cup(0, \pi/2]$ \\
$\arcsec x, \ \sec^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse secant, with domain $\R\setminus(-1, 1)$ and range $[0, \pi/2)\cup(\pi/2, \pi]$ \\
$\arccot x, \ \cot^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse cotangent, $\R\to(0, \pi)$ \\
$\sinh x$ & Hyperbolic sine \\
$\cosh x$ & Hyperbolic cosine \\
$\tanh x$ & Hyperbolic tangent \\
$\csch x$ & Hyperbolic cosecant \\
$\sech x$ & Hyperbolic secant \\
$\coth x$ & Hyperbolic cotangent \\
$\arcsinh x, \ \sinh^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse hyperbolic sine, $\R\to\R$ \\
$\arccosh x, \ \cosh^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse hyperbolic cosine, $[1, \infty)\to[0, \infty)$ \\
$\arctanh x, \ \tanh^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse hyperbolic tangent, $(-1, 1)\to\R$ \\
$\arccsch x, \ \csch^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse hyperbolic cosecant, $\R\setminus\{0\}\to\R\setminus\{0\}$ \\
$\arcsech x, \ \sech^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse hyperbolic secant, $(0, 1]\to[0, \infty)$ \\
$\arccoth x, \ \coth^{-1}x$ & Principal real inverse hyperbolic cotangent, $\R\setminus[-1, 1]\to\R\setminus\{0\}$ \\
$\Gamma(z)$ & Gamma function \\
$\psi(z)$ & Digamma function, $\psi(z)=\Gamma'(z)/\Gamma(z)$ \\
$B(x, y)$ & Beta function \\
$\gamma$ & Euler--Mascheroni constant \\
$\zeta(s)$ & Riemann zeta function \\
$(X, \mathcal{A}, \mu)$ & Measure space $X$ with $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{A}$ and measure $\mu$ \\
$\mathcal{A}$ & $\sigma$-algebra of measurable subsets of $X$, when the underlying set is understood \\
$\mathcal{B}(X)$ & Borel $\sigma$-algebra of a topological space $X$ \\
$\mu(E)$ & Measure of a measurable set $E\in\mathcal{A}$ \\
$\displaystyle\int_E f\,\dd\mu$ & Integral of $f$ over $E$ with respect to the measure $\mu$ \\
$(f\ast g)(x)$ & Convolution of functions $f$ and $g$ \\
$f=g\ \textnormal{a.e.}, \quad f=g\ \mu\textnormal{-a.e.}$ & Equality almost everywhere, with the measure omitted when understood \\
$\displaystyle\essinf_{x\in E} f(x)$ & Essential infimum of $f$ on $E$ \\*
$\displaystyle\esssup_{x\in E} f(x)$ & Essential supremum of $f$ on $E$ \\
$C(\Omega), \ C^0(\Omega)$ & Continuous functions on $\Omega$ \\*
$C^k(\Omega)$ & $k$ times continuously differentiable functions on $\Omega$ \\*
$C^\infty(\Omega)$ & Smooth functions on $\Omega$ \\
$C^k(\Omega; \R^m)$ & $C^k$ maps from $\Omega$ into $\R^m$ \\
$C_b(X)$ & Bounded continuous scalar-valued functions on $X$ \\
$\operatorname{supp}f$ & Support of $f$ \\
$C_c(\Omega)$ & Continuous functions on $\Omega$ with compact support \\
$C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ & Smooth functions on $\Omega$ with compact support \\
$V_a^b(f)$ & Total variation of $f$ on $[a, b]$ \\
$\mathrm{BV}([a, b])$ & Functions of bounded variation on $[a, b]$ \\
$\mathcal{R}([a, b])$ & Functions Riemann integrable on $[a, b]$ \\*
$\mathcal{R}([a, b], \alpha)$ & Functions Riemann--Stieltjes integrable on $[a, b]$ with respect to $\alpha$ \\
$\|f\|_\infty$ & Supremum norm of a bounded scalar-valued function $f$ \\
$L^p(\Omega), \ L^p(X, \mu)$ & Lebesgue $L^p$ spaces on $\Omega$ or $(X, \mu)$, modulo almost-everywhere equality \\*
$L^\infty(\Omega), \ L^\infty(X, \mu)$ & Essentially bounded measurable functions on $\Omega$ or $(X, \mu)$, modulo almost-everywhere equality \\
$\|f\|_{L^p(\Omega)}, \ \|f\|_{L^p(X, \mu)}, \ \|f\|_p$ & $L^p$-norm, for $1\leq p<\infty$, on $\Omega$ or $(X, \mu)$ \\
$\|f\|_{L^\infty(\Omega)}, \ \|f\|_{L^\infty(X, \mu)}, \ \|f\|_\infty$ & Essential supremum norm on $\Omega$ or $(X, \mu)$ in the $L^\infty$ context \\
$\ell^p$ & Space of $p$-summable scalar sequences \\
$\ell^\infty$ & Space of bounded scalar sequences \\
$\|x\|_{\ell^p}$ & $\ell^p$-norm of the sequence $x$, for $1\leq p<\infty$ \\*
$\|x\|_{\ell^\infty}$ & Infinity norm of a bounded scalar sequence $x$ \\
$B_r(x)$ & Open ball of radius $r$ centered at $x$ \\*
$\overline{B}_r(x)=\{y\mid d(x, y)\leq r\}$ & Closed ball of radius $r$ centered at $x$; not necessarily the closure of $B_r(x)$ \\
$\widehat{\C}=\C\cup\{\infty\}$ & Extended complex plane, equivalently the Riemann sphere \\
$i^2=-1$ & Imaginary unit \\
$z=x+iy$ & Complex number with real part $x$ and imaginary part $y$ \\
$\RePart z, \ \Re z$ & Real part of $z$ \\
$\ImPart z, \ \Im z$ & Imaginary part of $z$ \\
$\overline{z}$ & Complex conjugate \\
$|z|$ & Complex modulus \\
$\arg z$ & Argument of a nonzero complex number $z$; its values differ by integer multiples of $2\pi$ \\
$\Arg z$ & Principal argument of $z\ne0$, with $-\pi<\Arg z\leq\pi$ \\
$\log z, \ \ln z$ & General multivalued complex logarithm for $z\ne0$; $\displaystyle\log z=\{\ln|z|+i(\Arg z+2\pi k)\mid k\in\Z\}$ \\
$\Log z, \ \Ln z$ & Principal complex logarithm for $z\ne0$; $\displaystyle\Log z=\ln|z|+i\Arg z$. On $\C\setminus(-\infty, 0]$, it is the analytic principal branch, with $-\pi<\Arg z<\pi$ \\
$\mathcal{O}(U)$ & Holomorphic functions on an open set $U\subseteq\C$ \\
$\ord_a(f)$ & Order of a nonzero meromorphic function $f$ at $a$; positive for a zero and negative for a pole \\
$\Res(f; a), \ \displaystyle\Res_{z=a}f(z)$ & Residue of $f$ at a finite point $a$ \\
$\Res(f; \infty), \ \displaystyle\Res_{z=\infty}f(z)$ & Residue of $f$ at infinity \\
