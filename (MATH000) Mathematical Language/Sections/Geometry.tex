\section{Geometry}

Uppercase letters such as $A,B,C$ denote points. In triangle notation, $a,b,c$ denote the side lengths opposite $A,B,C$, respectively.

\begin{notationtable}
$\triangle ABC$ & Triangle with vertices $A,B,C$. \\
$\angle ABC$ & Angle with vertex $B$. \\
$\overline{AB}$ & Segment joining $A$ and $B$. \\
$AB$ & Length of $\overline{AB}$. \\
$[ABC]$ & Area of $\triangle ABC$. \\
$\lengthop(C),\ \ell(C)$ & Length of $C$. \\
$\area(S)$ & Area of $S$. \\
$\vol(S)$ & Volume of $S$. \\
$\dist(P,S)$ & Distance from $P$ to $S$. \\
$\conv(S)$ & Convex hull of $S$. \\
$\mathbb{E}^n$ & $n$-dimensional Euclidean space. \\
$\mathbb{H}^n$ & $n$-dimensional hyperbolic space. \\
$\parallel$ & Parallel. \\
$\perp$ & Perpendicular or orthogonal. \\
\end{notationtable}
