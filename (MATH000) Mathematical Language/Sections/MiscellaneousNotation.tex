\section{Miscellaneous Notation}

\begin{notationtable}
$\lfloor x\rfloor$ & Floor of $x$. \\
$\lceil x\rceil$ & Ceiling of $x$. \\
$\{x\}$ & Fractional part $x-\lfloor x\rfloor$, when unambiguous. \\
$\sgn(x)$ & Sign of a real number or permutation, according to context. \\
$a\propto b$ & $a$ is proportional to $b$; in a functional context, the proportionality factor is independent of the variable under discussion. \\
$O(g(n))$ & Big-O upper asymptotic bound. \\
$o(g(n))$ & Little-o strict asymptotic bound. \\
$\Theta(g(n))$ & Two-sided asymptotic order. \\
$\Omega(g(n))$ & Asymptotic lower bound. \\
$f(x)\asymp g(x)$ & Comparable up to positive multiplicative constants in a stated regime: $c\,g(x)\le f(x)\le C\,g(x)$ for some $0<c\le C$ and nonnegative $g$. \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}} f(a,b,c)$ & Cyclic sum over the cyclic permutations of the displayed variables; for three variables, $f(a,b,c)+f(b,c,a)+f(c,a,b)$. \\
$\displaystyle\sum_{\mathrm{sym}} f(a,b,c)$ & Symmetric sum over the distinct permutations of the displayed variables in the summand; the variables and any normalization are stated when needed. \\
$\Delta q$ & Finite change in a quantity $q$, especially in a discrete or applied context. \\
$\delta_{ij}$ & Kronecker delta: $1$ if $i=j$ and $0$ otherwise. \\
$\epsilon_{ijk}$ & Three-dimensional Levi--Civita symbol: the sign of the permutation $(i,j,k)$ when $(i,j,k)$ is a permutation of $(1,2,3)$, and $0$ otherwise. \\
\end{notationtable}
