\section{Probability and Statistics}

Probability and expectation use blackboard-bold operators.

\begin{notationtable}
$\Prob(A),\ P(A)$ & Probability of event $A$. \\
$\Prob(A\mid B),\ P(A\mid B)$ & Conditional probability of $A$ given $B$. \\
$\Exp[X],\ E[X]$ & Expectation of random variable $X$. \\
$\Var(X)$ & Variance of $X$. \\
$\Cov(X,Y)$ & Covariance of $X$ and $Y$. \\
$X\sim\Bin(n,p)$ & Binomial distribution. \\
$X\sim\Poi(\lambda)$ & Poisson distribution. \\
$X\sim\Normal(\mu,\sigma^2)$ & Normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. \\
$X_n\asto X$ & Almost-sure convergence. \\
$X_n\probto X$ & Convergence in probability. \\
$X_n\Lpto{p}X$ & Convergence in $L^p$. \\
$X_n\distto X,\ X_n\Rightarrow X$ & Convergence in distribution. \\
\end{notationtable}
