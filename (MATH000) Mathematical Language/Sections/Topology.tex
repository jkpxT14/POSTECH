\section{Topology}

\begin{notationtable}
$(X,\mathcal T)$ & Topological space $X$ with topology $\mathcal T$. \\
$\mathcal T$ & Topology on the underlying set $X$, when $X$ is understood. \\
$\mathcal B$ & Basis for a topology. \\
$\mathcal S$ & Subbasis for a topology. \\
$A^\circ,\ \interior A$ & Interior of $A$. \\*
$\overline A,\ \closure A$ & Closure of $A$. \\
$\partial A$ & Boundary of $A$. \\
$(X,d)$ & Metric space $X$ with metric $d$. \\
$\mathcal T_A=\{A\cap U\mid U\in\mathcal T\}$ & Subspace topology on $A\subseteq X$. \\
$\displaystyle\pi_i\colon\prod_{j\in J}X_j\to X_i$ & Coordinate projection of a product space, for $i\in J$. \\
$X/{\sim}$ & Quotient space when the quotient set is equipped with the quotient topology. \\
$q\colon X\twoheadrightarrow X/{\sim}$ & Canonical quotient map. \\
$X/A$ & Quotient space obtained by collapsing the subspace $A\subseteq X$ to a point. \\
$C(X,Y)$ & Set of continuous maps from $X$ to $Y$. \\
$X\cong Y$ & Homeomorphic spaces. \\
$\Homeo(X)$ & Group of self-homeomorphisms of $X$. \\
$\iota\colon A\hookrightarrow X$ & Inclusion map of a subspace $A\subseteq X$. \\
$f\colon X\hookrightarrow Y$ & Topological embedding when explicitly stated; the hook arrow by itself retains its general meaning of injectivity. \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
$(X,x_0)$ & Pointed space with basepoint $x_0$. \\
$I=[0,1]$ & Unit interval, especially for paths and homotopies. \\
$\gamma\colon I\to X$ & Path in $X$. \\
$\gamma(0)=\gamma(1)=x_0$ & Loop based at $x_0$. \\
$H\colon X\times I\to Y$ & Homotopy between maps $X\to Y$. \\
$f\simeq g$ & Homotopic maps $f,g\colon X\to Y$. \\
$X\simeq Y$ & Homotopy equivalent spaces. \\
$[\gamma]$ & Homotopy class of a path or loop $\gamma$, according to context. \\
$\pi_1(X,x_0)$ & Fundamental group of $X$ based at $x_0$. \\
$\pi_n(X,x_0)$ & $n$th homotopy group of the pointed space $(X,x_0)$, for $n\ge1$. \\
$f_*$ & Homomorphism induced by a map $f$ on a functorial invariant such as homotopy or homology, with the invariant clear from context. \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
$p\colon\widetilde X\to X$ & Covering map, with $\widetilde X$ a covering space of $X$. \\
$\widetilde X$ & Covering space; it denotes a universal cover only when explicitly specified. \\
$\Deck(p)$ & Group of deck transformations of the covering $p$. \\
$D^n=\{\mathbf x\in\R^n\mid\|\mathbf x\|\le1\}$ & Closed $n$-disk. \\
$X\vee Y$ & Wedge sum of pointed spaces $X$ and $Y$. \\
$T_0,T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4$ & Separation axioms; here $T_2$ means Hausdorff, $T_3$ means $T_1$ and regular, and $T_4$ means $T_1$ and normal. \\
$M^n$ & $n$-dimensional topological manifold, when the dimension is displayed. \\
$\partial M$ & Boundary of a manifold $M$. \\
$\Sigma_g$ & Closed connected orientable surface of genus $g$. \\
$M\# N$ & Connected sum of manifolds $M$ and $N$. \\
$\chi(X)$ & Euler characteristic of $X$. \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
$H_n(X;G)$ & $n$th homology group of $X$ with coefficients in $G$. \\
$H_n(X)$ & Integral homology group $H_n(X;\Z)$ when coefficients are omitted. \\
$\widetilde H_n(X;G)$ & $n$th reduced homology group of $X$ with coefficients in $G$. \\
$H^n(X;G)$ & $n$th cohomology group of $X$ with coefficients in $G$. \\
$b_n(X)$ & $n$th Betti number; for example, $b_n(X)=\dim_{\Q}H_n(X;\Q)$ when this dimension is finite. \\
\end{notationtable}
