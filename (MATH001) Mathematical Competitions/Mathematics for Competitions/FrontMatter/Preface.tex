This book is intended to help students prepare for mathematical competitions,
from middle- and high-school olympiads to undergraduate competitions. It
brings together mathematical theory and problem-solving methods that are
especially useful for approaching challenging competition problems.

The book is organized into two parts. \emph{Mathematical Concepts} develops
relevant definitions, theorems, proofs, and applications, including advanced
theory where it is useful. \emph{Problem-Solving Strategies} focuses on methods
of proof, general approaches, and practical techniques for solving difficult
problems. The emphasis is on understanding not only what is true, but also
why it is true and how it can be used.

This book is a companion to \emph{Mathematics for Science Quiz}, which places
greater emphasis on important results, memorable formulas, mathematical
history, and interesting facts and connections. Readers looking for a broader
collection of such knowledge may consult that volume; the present book
provides a more detailed treatment of proofs, deeper theory, and mathematical
problem solving. The two books are intended to complement, rather than
replace, one another.

I hope this book helps readers develop mathematical insight, creativity,
and perseverance, and encourages them to aim for the highest achievements
in mathematical competitions.
