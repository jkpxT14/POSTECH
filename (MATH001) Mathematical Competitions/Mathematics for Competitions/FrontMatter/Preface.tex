This book is intended to help students prepare for mathematical competitions,
from middle- and high-school olympiads to undergraduate competitions. It
brings together mathematical concepts, results, and problem-solving methods
that are useful in approaching challenging competition problems.

The book is organized into two parts. \emph{Mathematical Concepts} collects
definitions, theorems, formulas, and other mathematical results that can be
applied in competitions, from elementary foundations to more advanced theory.
These are presented without proofs so that the essential statements and their
conditions remain easy to consult. \emph{Problem-Solving Strategies} is devoted
to methods of proof, mathematical ideas, and techniques for solving difficult
problems, including instructive proofs where appropriate.

This book is a companion to \emph{Mathematics for Science Quiz}, which places
greater emphasis on important results, memorable formulas, mathematical
history, and interesting facts and connections. The two books may share
fundamental definitions and theorems, but they emphasize different purposes:
that volume offers broad mathematical knowledge, while this one focuses on
the tools and reasoning used in mathematical competitions. Readers may consult
either volume independently or use them together.

I hope this book helps readers develop mathematical insight, creativity,
and perseverance, and encourages them to aim for the highest achievements
in mathematical competitions.
